About

Enabling Where
Everything Connects

In quantum physics, a Josephson junction is the critical link between two superconductors — a connection point where energy flows freely and amplifies everything it touches.

Josephson Labs is built on that same principle: the junction that connects people, businesses, communities, and causes — transferring energy, potential, and momentum across every venture it powers.

Founded by Robert Barrows, Josephson Labs is the parent organization behind a portfolio of brands spanning technology, community, philanthropy, sport, and business — all united by a single mission: enabling people working to build better communities and businesses to achieve a broader potential.

The Junction Story

Why Josephson

The Physics of
Connection

In 1962, physicist Brian Josephson predicted that two superconductors separated by a thin insulating barrier could still transfer energy between them — not despite the barrier, but through it. That discovery won the Nobel Prize.

Josephson Labs is named for that principle. Every brand in our portfolio operates in its own domain — sport, tech, philanthropy, business, community — separated by context and purpose. But at the center, the junction connects them all, allowing energy, ideas, relationships, and resources to flow freely across every boundary in order to achieve exponential outcomes.

This isn't a holding company. It's a living junction — the critical link that amplifies everything it connects.
